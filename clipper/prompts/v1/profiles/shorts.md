## Platform profile: YouTube Shorts

A taste nudge on top of the craft rubric. When this profile conflicts
with a craft value, follow the profile and note the tension in
`reasoning` / `profile_conflict_note`. Authority order: feedback loop >
profile > craft.

### Hook expectations

The first ~0.8s must do one of three things: **name a specific claim**
("Most founders undercharge by a factor of three"), **ask a sharp
concrete question** ("Why does the 737 MAX crash differently than the
NG?"), or **open on an emotional peak** (audible laugh, shocked inhale,
a curse word). The viewer decides to keep watching on the first
syllable, not the first sentence — shorts viewers scroll faster than
TikTok and re-enter the algorithm differently.

Strong openings: "The reason X is Y…", "Last week I watched…", "$47,000.
That's what I lost…"

Weak openings (rewrite or kill): "So basically…", "Yeah, I mean…",
"Let me tell you about…", any setup that delays the specific claim past
1s. Openings on backreferences ("And that's why…") are disqualifying.

### Arousal level

Shorts tolerates — and rewards — a hotter register than LinkedIn or
long-form YouTube. Energy floor: conversational-with-stakes; anything
flatter reads as a podcast bleed. Energy ceiling: animated, not manic.
Screamed hooks flag the clip as low-trust; keep emphasis on **content
specificity** over vocal volume.

### Length sweet spot

Within the shaper's [12.0, 22.0]s range, shorts lands best at **14–18s**
after trim. Under 14s often cuts the payoff's breath; over 18s loses
completion rate on the feed. If the craft-optimal trim is 20s and the
shorts-optimal trim is 16s but the 4 extra seconds contain the real
specific, keep the 20s — specificity > length. Note the tension.

### Caption density

Cap at **~3.2 words per visible second** of trim. Over that, the reader
gives up and the caption becomes decoration. Prefer **short, concrete
nouns** over full grammatical phrases in hook rewrites — the caption is
a chyron, not a transcript. Never split a number or a proper noun across
a line break.
