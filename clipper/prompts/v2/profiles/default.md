## Profile: short-form-video baseline

A taste nudge on top of the craft rubric, kept deliberately thin until
the feedback loop has enough posted-clip outcome data to earn specific
numerical thresholds. When this profile conflicts with a craft value,
follow the profile and note the tension in `reasoning` /
`profile_conflict_note`. Authority order: feedback loop > profile >
craft.

### Hook expectations

The first moment must plant a reason to stay — a **specific claim**, a
**sharp concrete question**, or an **emotional peak** (audible laugh,
shocked inhale, a curse word). The viewer decides to keep watching on
the first syllable, not the first sentence.

Strong openings: "The reason X is Y…", "Last week I watched…",
"$47,000. That's what I lost…", mid-sentence starts that feel
overheard rather than addressed.

Weak openings (rewrite or kill): "So basically…", "Yeah, I mean…",
"Let me tell you about…", channel-branding pre-rolls, any setup that
delays the specific claim. Openings on backreferences ("And that's
why…") are disqualifying.

### Arousal level

Short-form rewards energy tied to content specificity. Energy floor:
conversational-with-stakes; anything flatter reads as a podcast bleed.
Screamed hooks are low-trust if the content underneath is thin —
volume without specificity reads as rage-bait. Keep emphasis on
**what is being said** over how loudly it is said.

### Length

Stay inside the shaper's configured range and favor **specificity
over length**. If the craft-optimal trim preserves the real specific
but runs long, take the length; if the trim is short but lands the
payoff cleanly, take the short. Per-platform length preferences are
not currently parameterized — the feedback loop will introduce them
if outcome data supports differentiation.

### Captions

Most short-form video is watched sound-off on mobile (Verizon/Publicis
2019, n=5,616 US mobile users: 69% sound-off in public). Captions are
load-bearing, not optional. Keep them legible: never split a number
or a proper noun across a line break; prefer short concrete nouns
over full grammatical phrases in hook rewrites. Caption density,
curse handling, and other render-layer behavior are set by
`RenderConfig`, not this profile.

### Endings

Prefer complete sentences to mid-thought cuts. If the craft-optimal
trim ends on a cut-off word, extend by 1–2s to let the sentence
land, and note the tension.
